TRAVEL SMART

Travel Tips

  • Use Public Transport

    Trains, buses and metro systems can make travelling around French cities easier.

  • Try Local Food

    Small local cafés and bakeries can be a great part of the French experience.

  • Learn Basic French Phrases

    Knowing a few basic phrases can go a long way in making connections with locals.

  • Explore Beyond Tourist Spots

    Take some time to walk through quieter streets and discover less crowded places.

  • Plan Before You Go

    Check opening hours, transport routes and booking requirements before visiting.
